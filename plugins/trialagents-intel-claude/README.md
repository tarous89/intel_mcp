# TrialAgents - European Clinical Trial Intelligence

Clinical-trial research for biotech and clinical-operations teams. Ask Claude to find and compare clinical sites, principal investigators (PIs) and CROs for a study across Europe, explore trial landscapes, and read protocols, eligibility criteria and endpoints. Results open in an interactive workspace inside the conversation, with Sites, PIs, CROs, Trials and Custom tabs.

## What you can do

- **Site selection:** "Find sites in Germany for a phase 3 lung cancer study."
- **Investigator search:** "Find PIs with phase 3 Alzheimer's disease trial experience in Europe."
- **CRO selection:** "List the CROs with regulatory submission experience from phase 2 pediatric clinical trials."
- **Protocol review:** "Read the protocol of the first trial and summarize the primary endpoint."
- **Charts, reports and trackers:** "Create comparison cards for European lung cancer trials studying antibody-drug conjugates." Custom views are saved to your TrialAgents project.
- **Saved projects:** "Show my saved projects" reopens earlier research, including projects created on the TrialAgents website.

The plugin adds one skill, **Ask TrialAgents**, and the TrialAgents Intel connector. Large searches use the most recent matching trials so you always get a list, and each answer links to the saved project on the TrialAgents website.

## Access

Research works without an account: free access shows the top ten results per table. Saving projects and Custom views requires signing in to a TrialAgents account through the connector. Full result lists and expanded trial content require an active paid TrialAgents account, purchased on the TrialAgents website; the plugin starts no purchase.

## Data and privacy

The plugin contains instructions only and runs no local code. It connects to one service, the TrialAgents Intel MCP server at `https://mcp.trialagents.com/claude/mcp`, operated by Atlas Consulting. Your research questions, search criteria and the content you ask to save (projects, table views, charts and reports) are sent to that server. When you are signed in, saved projects and Custom views are stored in your TrialAgents account; guest previews are kept for seven days. Reading trial profiles and documents records which sources were read, for report provenance.

Results describe recorded clinical-trial registry data (EU CTIS). Trial participation does not establish recruitment capacity, provider performance or availability. Do not enter identifiable patient information.
