Trial design comparison

Illustrative layout only — synthetic evidence from 10 accessible trials.

Executive summary

The example cohort favors randomized designs, while eligibility differences warrant separate subgroup review.

Which study designs are most common?

Seven of the 10 example trials are randomized; two are single-arm and one has no recorded design.

Nuances and implications

Design frequency does not establish which design is appropriate for a new protocol; compare objectives and populations before drawing conclusions.

Which eligibility differences need attention?

Prior treatment and biomarker requirements should be reviewed separately before combining the example populations.

Nuances and additional findings

Do not treat absence in a profile as proof that the protocol has no restriction.

Scope and limitations

This is a synthetic ten-trial example demonstrating structure, not clinical evidence. Actual reports must state the accessible population, snapshot and missingness. Source identifiers belong in saved metadata unless requested.
