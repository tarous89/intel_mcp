Clinical trials

Connect your agent

  1. ChatGPT: use the TrialAgents Intel app when available in your workspace. For a custom MCP connection, enter the research MCP URL above in your client's supported connector setup.
  2. Claude or another MCP client: add a remote server using the research MCP URL and Streamable HTTP. Client support and account requirements depend on the host.
  3. Own software: initialize an MCP client against the research endpoint, discover its tools and schemas, and use the advertised authentication flow when an operation requires an account.
  4. Connect your TrialAgents account when prompted: sign in with your existing account or create one. Continue the same research and open saved projects in TrialAgents Intel.

Each research selection supports up to 1,000 trials. The public preview shows up to 10 trials, sites, principal investigators and CROs each. Saving projects requires a connected account. Full matching lists and other paid capabilities depend on your account access. Connecting an account alone does not unlock paid access.

Agents should discover the live tool schemas, respect access limits and distinguish missing evidence from negative findings. Trial participation and recorded roles do not establish provider performance.

Useful details

What does TrialAgents cover?

TrialAgents documents all details about every clinical trial with medicinal products approved in the EU since 2021, with all information collected and structured. The research connection brings that evidence into supported agent workflows. Individual document retrieval depends on document availability and account access.

Where do I log in and find my projects?

Open your projects in TrialAgents Intel. If needed, you will be asked to log in through the existing TrialAgents sign-in flow.

Is this page the MCP endpoint?

This is the human-readable documentation page. Configure your agent with https://mcp.trialagents.com/research/mcp. Existing integrations using the legacy endpoint https://mcp.trialagents.com/mcp remain supported; do not change an existing integration's endpoint without checking its tool requirements.

How is account access handled?

TrialAgents uses its existing OAuth connection flow. Your client requests authorization when required. Email hints help fill in the login form; they do not authenticate a user or grant access to another account's projects.
